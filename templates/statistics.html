OUTLIER DETECTION

Potential outliers by variable

Counts are calculated using the interquartile range (IQR) rule.

An outlier is a value outside Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or Q3 + 1.5 × IQR. It should be investigated rather than automatically deleted.
